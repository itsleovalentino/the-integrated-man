# The Integrated Man — North Star

> The soul of the app, in Leo's words (Aug 2026). Every future build gets measured against this.
> If a feature doesn't serve this, it doesn't belong — no matter how good it looks.

---

## The first three seconds

When a man opens this app, before any feature, he should be **reminded of who he's becoming and what matters most**:

- His walk with God — *seek first the Kingdom* (Matthew 6:33).
- Abiding in Christ (John 15).
- Becoming — growing — **while surrendering.** You as the vessel, God as the power.

And in that same breath, he should know **he is not alone.** His brothers are here, walking with him. Some have already gotten going on their day; some are behind. We are **not competing with one another — we are lifting each other up.** Each man on his own journey, but collectively carrying each other through.

## The third place

Every man has a *first place* (home) and a *second place* (work). What the modern man has lost is the **third place** — the space that grounds him, grows him, sharpens him, where community actually happens.

**The Integrated Man is that third place — and a digital one that *grounds* instead of drains.** The opposite of the feed. This is both the mission and the moat: almost every digital space ever built pulls a man out of presence; this one puts him back into it.

## It's a house, and the features are rooms

Not tabs. Rooms a man returns to:

- **The hearth** — the opening moment: reminded who he's becoming, the Word on the table, and the brothers already in the room (some ahead, some behind).
- **The Word room** — reading, abiding.
- **The private study** — journaling, audience of one.
- **The gym** — the pods, the becoming-actions.
- **The prayer room** — Fellowship, the covenant.
- **The workshop** — the Forge, building the vision.

> "They've got brothers, the Bible is on the table, a comfortable place to sit, but also the gym, a sauna, a prayer room, private spaces, places to work. It's the place they always want to go, and when they do, they grow. A place they can always come back to, and when they do, their walk with God increases."

## The soul stack

One organism, three layers:

1. **The walk with Jesus** — the center. The why.
2. **Becoming** (Word, journal, the seven pillars, the Forge) — the *work*.
3. **Accountability / pods** — what makes a man actually *do* the work, with brothers.

Accountability is the **hook** (what gets a man in the door and spreads by word of mouth), the **engine** (how a man actually changes), and the **growth flywheel** (a man brings his brothers) — all at once. It is the app's center of gravity. But it is never a standalone tracker: what makes it uncommon is *what* a man is held to (becoming like Christ, the pillars, a vow before God) and *who* holds him (brothers on the same mission).

## The ethic: carry, don't compete

"Bear one another's burdens" (Galatians 6:2). The only competition is a man against his own empty ring and against the thief — **man vs. thief, never man vs. man.** When a brother is behind, the space doesn't let you pass him; it invites you to lift him.

## The hardest guardrail: it releases, not captures

A true third place grounds a man and then **sends him back into his life more present** — you leave the chapel to go live, you leave the gym stronger. **The win is never time-in-app; it's the life lived out there.** TIM succeeds when a man opens it, is reminded and sharpened and carried, and then *closes it to go be an integrated man in his home, his work, his church, his city.*
