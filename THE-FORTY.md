# THE FORTY
### Program bible · v2 · July 2026
*A 40-day call to adventure inside The Integrated Man. Becoming the man God is calling you to be, through becoming and surrender. Personal Power, but you as the vessel and God as the power.*

*v2 is simulation-tested. Four men walked v1 day by day: Tyler (19, not yet a Christian, solo, porn and scroll battles), Marcus (27, lukewarm, quits things at week 2, doing it with two buddies), David (41, on fire, successful, already disciplined), and Jaylen (23, broke new believer, on fire, in a cohort of strangers). Their dropout maps, wince moments, and breakthrough moments rebuilt this program. Where v2 differs from v1, a simulation finding is the reason.*

---

## 1 · THE PROMISE (what a man has on Day 41)

By the end of The Forty a man has: a rhythm he runs on (the app's daily loop, now his), a body in motion, a mind with armor (a concrete protocol against the negative and insecure thoughts: catch it, name it, replace it with what is true), his identity spoken and written (a King's kid; a man of God; a spirit of power, love, and a sound mind, 2 Tim 1:7), one real confession behind him, one table he hosted, one older man speaking into him, one younger man he's pouring into, his money faced, his space ordered, his calling clearer, and a receipt: his Day 0 number next to his Day 38 number, and his Day 1 words read back to him. Some men meet God inside these 40 days. Some catch fire. Some finally hear their calling. The program makes room for all three and forces none.

## 2 · THE TEN DESIGN LAWS (every day is checked against these)

1. **Liturgy, not playlist.** Three constants only: the Word is present, one act of obedience, it gets marked. Everything else varies by day type (Charge, Descent, Field, Silence, Debrief, Gate, Sending). Sessions breathe from 40 seconds to 15 minutes.
2. **Sell before you ask.** Vision first (Willard's VIM). Day 0 opens with a 90-second call to adventure, not a form. Every hard day opens with why it's worth it.
3. **Never let a man descend unwitnessed.** The quit window is Days 15 to 20 (all four simulations). Those days auto-pulse his witnesses. Nobody enters the Descent alone.
4. **Route by the Mirror.** The Day 0 score routes every day into Base or Iron tier. Never tell a 5:15am man to kill the snooze; never tell a beginner to fast 24 hours. The strong man must bleed too, or he coasts to Day 40 untouched (David).
5. **Grace speaks first.** After any missed day or any fall, the app opens its mouth before shame does: next morning, first thing, Leo's voice: "That's what grace is for. Today is open. Move." Silence after a fall is how men are lost (Tyler, Day 8).
6. **Relative, not absolute.** Every cut is measured against HIS life. The broke man's $10 and the owner's $1,000 must cost the same. "Some of you will find waste. Some will find nothing left to waste. Both of you just did the same brave thing: you looked." (Jaylen, money week.)
7. **Peers only, where it counts.** Confession, the Table, the Ask: no mentees, no men you lead, no one you're impressive to. For the leader, discovering he has no peer IS the confession (David, Day 20).
8. **The app goes quiet sometimes.** Day 19 has no audio at all. In every simulation it was the day the man remembered. Protect it. Never gamify it.
9. **Receipts over sermons.** The Mirror delta, the letter read-back, the 40-strike nail: evidence converts men who argue with claims. Surface the Day 38 scoreboard as the carrot from Day 22 onward (Marcus).
10. **End in sending.** Day 40 without multiplication is a cul-de-sac. One man invites his man. A leader invites his eight. 2 Tim 2:2 is the growth engine and the point.

## 3 · THE WITNESS SYSTEM (nobody does this alone, even alone)

Chosen at Day 0, changeable anytime. Every social day in the map ships all four variants.

| Mode | Who | How it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Circle** | His real TIM circle | Covenant board, full pulses, Table in person |
| **Brothers** | 2 to 3 friends, 30-second setup | One shared code creates a micro board (Marcus's fix). "UP" texts become competition. They ARE the Day 33 Table |
| **Cohort** | A campaign wave of strangers | Ambient board + **cordmate pairing at Day 8**: each man is assigned one name who notices when his dot goes dark (Jaylen's fix). Confession = cordmate call. Table = video table with provided questions |
| **Solo** | Nobody yet | He names one **witness**: any human he'll text (a gym friend, a coworker; the witness doesn't need the app). Key actions send that one text. Confession = voice memo or face to face with the witness. Table = "buy one man a meal and ask one real question" (Tyler's fix) |

Descent days (17 to 20) auto-pulse the witness lane: "He's in deep water this week."

## 4 · THE TIER SYSTEM (any man, any stage, every run)

The Day 0 Mirror routes each day: **Base** (building the discipline) or **Iron** (the discipline exists; surrender it). A man can override either way. The Iron tier is not "more reps": it converts each day from performance to surrender, which is the only place the already-strong man has anything left to lose. Examples in the day map below. This is also the repeatability engine: run 2 on Iron is a different program than run 1 on Base.

## 5 · LANGUAGE RULES (truth without Christianese)

- First use of any inside word is defined in the same breath, in Leo's audio. "Covenant: your word, signed." "Sabbath, call it the debrief, the deload day."
- Verses are always read aloud in plain English with one sentence of story: "David wrote this after the worst thing he ever did got dragged into the light" (Ps 51). Never a bare reference (Jaylen was lost; Tyler tuned out).
- The word "sin" does not appear in minute one. Name the thing plainly: "the stuff you manage with scrolling and drinking."
- **The skeptic's line, Day 0, verbatim:** "If God isn't your thing yet, good. Stay. One rule in here: don't fake anything." Assumed faith repels; invited honesty converts (Tyler).
- Keep: Godfidence (cheesy but sticky), the nail, "done or not yet."
- Gym vocabulary is native and welcome: deload, audit, baseline, reps.

## 6 · THE MOMENTUM ENGINE (dopamine, honestly earned)

- **The forge strike.** Completing a day = one hammer strike; over 40 days the man watches a nail being forged on his Forty screen (ember burst, haptic, strike sound). The physical nail on his desk and the digital nail being forged are the same object. This is the streak flame of The Forty.
- **Tomorrow's tease.** Each completion reveals only tomorrow's title ("Day 18: The Grave"). Locked until first light. The drip is the discipline; curiosity does the reminding.
- **Movement badges** at each Gate; the board shows which movement each man is in.
- **The scoreboard carrot.** From Day 22 (mid-Mirror), the app counts down to Day 38: "16 days until the number testifies."
- **The UP competition** (Brothers/Circle modes): first man up wins the day; the app keeps score for bragging only.
- **The completion artifact:** a beautiful page: his 40 strikes, both Mirror rings, Day 1 words vs Day 40 words. Built to be shown. This is how he tells his friends (and it IS the marketing).
- **Weekend-aware calendar.** Heavy field days (Long Walk, Sweep, Build, Table) carry 48 to 72 hour windows and auto-land on weekends. A program that ignores a man's calendar loses him on a Tuesday (Marcus, David).

## 7 · GRACE MECHANICS + THE FALL PROTOCOL

- **3 grace days.** Taught in Leo's voice at Day 7, not in fine print: "Using one is obedience, not weakness. The man who won't take mercy hasn't understood the program yet" (Jaylen's legalism fix). A missed day NEVER resets the run.
- **Grace speaks first.** Morning after any miss, before he can spiral: "Yesterday's gone. Day N is open. Move."
- **The Fall Protocol.** A hidden, always-available emergency session (3 minutes, Leo's voice) for the 1am fall: porn, the binge, the blowup. No shame spiral: get up, drink water, one text to your witness ("fell, getting up"), sleep. Next morning the app speaks first. The men most likely to be transformed by The Forty are the men most likely to fall during it; the program must be BUILT for the fall, not surprised by it (Tyler's Day 8 death was shame + silence, not the miss itself).
- **Standing rules cap: 3.** Only the phone rule, daily sweat, and the sleep hour persist as standing rules; everything else is day-scoped. Each Debrief offers keep or release. v1 stacked rules until they collided with a weekend and ate all three grace days by Day 13 (Marcus).

## 8 · THE ARC: DAY 0 + SIX MOVEMENTS

Separation → wilderness → return (van Gennep, Rohr). The low point past the middle, then the Naming, then the rise (Ignatius). Faith is the daily spine; the movements carry the other six pillars.

**DAY 0 · THE THRESHOLD** (target: under 10 minutes; v1 took 35 and lost men before Day 1)
1. The call to adventure: 90 seconds of Leo. What these 40 days are, what they cost, what's on the other side. The skeptic's line.
2. **The Mirror** (2 min): his Integration number. Immediately shareable ("send your number to your boys": Marcus's actual hook). Routes his tiers.
3. The covenant: one line, one signature. "Your word, signed."
4. Witness mode picked (Circle / Brothers / Cohort / Solo + witness name).
5. The letter: 3 starter prompts, "three sentences is enough," voice memo allowed, 5-minute cap.
6. **The nail quest:** find one nail. Garage, hardware store, the street. A dime-store nail beats no nail; a stone works if there's truly none. "Pending" is a valid state; Day 2 nudges. **Its meaning is NOT explained until Day 21.** The delayed reveal is the play (Tyler kept a "random nail" by his monitor and fidgeted with it at 1am; when it detonates at the Naming, it detonates retroactively).

## 9 · THE REPEAT ENGINE (a book, not a movie)

- **The letter chain.** Each run's Day 40 letter becomes the next run's Day 0 reading. By run 3 a man has a correspondence with himself across years. This alone makes rerunning irresistible (David).
- **Veteran callbacks.** "Last year at the Grave you burned [X]. Did it stay dead?" The app remembers; the program gets more personal every run.
- **Mirror overlay.** Multi-run rings on one chart: his becoming, visualized across years.
- **Tier progression.** Run 1 Base, run 2 Iron, run 3+ leading others: three different programs in the same liturgy.
- **The rotating spotlight.** Each rerun deepens one movement with alternate Iron days (write these later; the skeleton supports it).

## 10 · LEADER MODE (the multiplication engine)

- Completing a run unlocks leading one: the badge is the credential.
- A leader gets a shepherd view of his cohort's board and a one-page guide per movement.
- **Leaders need a room above them:** a man cannot confess (Day 20) to men he leads. Leaders-of-cohorts get their own circle. Protect the leader's honesty and he brings his eight (David: "not a user, a franchise").

## 11 · BUILD DELTA (v1 → v2 cost, honest)

- Witness modes: mostly UI + one pairing job (cordmates) + SMS/share-sheet for solo witness texts.
- Tiers: content roughly ×1.5 (most days share audio and fork only the action text; only ~8 days need distinct Iron audio).
- The Fall Protocol: 1 always-available audio + trigger UX. Small build, outsized retention.
- Forge-strike animation: the app already has celebration infrastructure (embers, chimes, haptics).
- Weekend-aware windows: scheduling logic on field days.
- Scripts: ~46 audio pieces total (40 days + fall protocol + gates variants). Still 3 to 5 hours of finished phone audio, batched.
